Planning to build on a plot? Start with the land, not just the building design.

Before construction begins, there are several important things to understand about your site:
- Are the plot boundaries properly assessed?
- What are the existing ground levels and slopes?
- Where are the drains, access points and other relevant features?
- Does your architect have the survey information needed for planning?
- Have you selected the right survey deliverables?

Boundary demarcation and topographic surveying serve different purposes. Understanding the difference can help property owners, developers and civil engineers define the right scope before work begins.
