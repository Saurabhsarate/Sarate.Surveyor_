Who We Are

Pioneers in Precision Surveying

Established in 1996, Sarate Surveyor has evolved into one of India's most trusted and technologically advanced land surveying agencies. Headquartered in Pune, Maharashtra, we have spent nearly three decades mastering the art and science of geospatial data collection, topographical mapping, and complex infrastructure alignment. Our core philosophy revolves around absolute precision, uncompromising integrity, and utilizing the latest advancements in surveying technology.

Over the years, our operations have scaled across the entire Indian subcontinent. We take immense pride in being the backbone of critical national infrastructure projects—partnering with prestigious Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs) like HPCL, IOCL, and BPCL, as well as municipal corporations and leading private developers. From fixing complex legal property boundaries to routing cross-country high-pressure gas pipelines, our team of licensed surveyors delivers court-admissible, centimeter-level accurate deliverables.

Our Expertise

Comprehensive Surveying Solutions

🔥

Gas Pipeline Survey

We conduct exhaustive end-to-end route planning and alignment mapping for high-pressure cross-country oil and gas pipelines. Adhering strictly to HPCL, IOCL, and BPCL regulatory standards, our surveys include detailed L-Section (Longitudinal) and Cross-Section profiling, clash detection with existing underground utilities, and accurate ROU (Right of Use) corridor demarcation to ensure seamless pipeline laying operations.

Learn More →
💧

Water Pipeline Survey

Our water infrastructure surveys encompass precise alignment, hydraulic gradient tracking, and elevation mapping critical for municipal water supply networks, large-scale irrigation canals, and long-distance gravity-fed transmission pipelines. We provide meticulously detailed CAD drawings that help engineering teams optimize flow dynamics, design pumping stations, and minimize excavation costs.

Learn More →
📐

Boundary Survey

As a Government-approved and registered surveying agency, our boundary demarcation services carry immense legal validity. We specialize in resolving complex land disputes, accurate plot sub-division, super-imposition of village maps (Gat/Survey numbers), and exact area calculations. Our digitally authenticated boundary reports are widely accepted by revenue departments and local municipal authorities.

Learn More →
🏔️

Topographic Survey

We capture high-resolution, centimeter-accurate topographical data to generate highly detailed contour maps and 3D digital terrain models (DTM). This service is indispensable for architects, town planners, and civil engineers executing large-scale housing developments, industrial parks, or commercial layouts. Our deliverables include plotting of all existing natural and man-made features to facilitate flawless project planning.

Learn More →
🛣️

Road Survey

Sarate Surveyor delivers comprehensive geometric design surveys for national highways, state roads, and urban streets. Our deliverables include high-density L-sections, transverse cross-sections, camber analysis, and precise earthwork (cut and fill) volume calculations. We ensure that your road alignment meets all IRC (Indian Roads Congress) standards for gradients, curves, and drainage.

Learn More →
🛰️

DGPS & Total Station

Leveraging state-of-the-art satellite-based Real-Time Kinematic (RTK) DGPS receivers and robotic Total Stations, we establish highly accurate primary and secondary control points. This millimeter-level precision is deployed for complex structural alignments, construction staking of high-rise building grids, monitoring structural deformation, and geo-referencing massive infrastructure sites with global coordinate systems.

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of surveys do you specialize in? +

We specialize in Gas Pipeline, Water Pipeline, Boundary Demarcation, Topographic contour mapping, Road surveys, and high-precision DGPS & Total Station control establishment.

Do you serve outside Pune? +

Yes, while our head office is located in Pune, Maharashtra, we have the capability and team strength to handle surveying projects anywhere across India.

Are you government registered? +

Absolutely. We are a Government Approved agency, Udyam Registered Micro-Enterprise, and hold valid surveyor licenses. Our deliverables are fully compliant with legal authorities.

How long does a typical survey take? +

The timeline depends on the project scope and terrain. A basic boundary survey takes 1-2 days, while large pipeline or topographic projects can span weeks. Contact us for a specific estimate.

What deliverables do you provide? +

We provide physical stamped drawings, detailed AutoCAD (DWG) files, PDF reports, coordinate lists (CSV/Excel), and 3D terrain models if requested.

Do you offer free consultations? +

Yes, we offer free initial consultations and site feasibility discussions. Get in touch with our team to evaluate your project needs and receive a no-obligation quote.
